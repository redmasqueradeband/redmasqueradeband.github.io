I started keeping this list privately around 2006 after a few reports crossed paths that I didn't think should be a coincidence. I made it public in 2011 after the third one. I'm not claiming these are connected, some might just be old coatings or paints nobody's documented properly. I'm saying I've catalogued them because somebody should, and because every other place this was written down has a way of disappearing. (The "A247" designation, if you're wondering, comes from a stamp found on the earliest board I could get clear photos of — A-247, no other markings, nothing that matches any registry I've checked.)

Traits that show up across every confirmed entry: a dim violet glow that does not fade with time and doesn't correspond to any closed circuit anyone's been able to measure. A faint hum, described consistently by people who never spoke to each other. A pulsing or "breathing" rhythm to the glow. The board is always the only object recovered fully intact from wherever it was found — crate, cellar, toolbox, doesn't matter. And in every case I've been able to follow closely, the original poster goes quiet within a few weeks of the thread being locked.

I want to be careful here. I am not saying anything happened to anyone. I don't have proof of that and I won't speculate about it in public. I'm saying the pattern is the pattern, and if you're on this list, I'd rather you know that than not.

closing note

Fourteen that I can prove. I suspect more that I can't. The gap between entries has been getting shorter, not longer, and I don't have a clean explanation for that either.

If you're reading this because your board just started doing this: stop reading, document what you can — both sides, in daylight and in the dark — and message me directly rather than posting publicly. Don't just gaze at it. Document it, then step away. Don't wait a few days to see if it settles down. None of the entries above waited, and it didn't matter.
